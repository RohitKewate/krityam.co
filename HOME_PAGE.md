## 11. FAQ
- **What is RTO and why does it matter?** → Return to Origin: orders that are shipped but come back undelivered. Each one costs you the ad spend, the outbound shipping, the return shipping and the sale.
- **Do I need to change my store or courier?** → No. We work with your current setup and cover the details in the audit.
- **Will AI calls hurt the customer experience?** → The calls are short and specific to the customer's own order. Sensitive cases go to a human.
- **How much of my team's time does this need?** → One onboarding session and a weekly 30-minute call. We do the rest.
- **What does the free audit include?** → An analysis of your RTO, COD and delivery data, your estimated revenue lost and the patterns driving it.
